# Sugar Light

## Short description
Cut added sugar with a personal daily plan, calm craving resets, and honest progress.

## Full description
Sugar Light helps adults reduce added sugar one choice at a time.

START WITH YOUR REAL ROUTINE
Tell Sugar Light which sugary food or drink shows up most often, roughly what it costs, when cravings tend to arrive, and why you want to change. Your first plan starts from those answers instead of a generic lecture.

MAKE ONE SATISFYING SWAP
Keep one concrete alternative ready for the moment that usually catches you. The goal is not a perfect diet. It is making the next choice easier.

LOG WITHOUT STARTING OVER
Log added sugar in neutral language and undo mistakes. One item does not erase the day or your earlier choices. Estimates update from the assumptions you entered and are labeled as estimates.

RESET A CRAVING
Open the 60-second reset, follow the calm circle, drink water, walk for two minutes, or return to your chosen swap. Sugar Light creates a pause, then helps you choose again.

SEE THE PATTERN CHANGE
Track sugar-light time, items skipped, and estimated spend kept. An evening check-in remembers what helped and carries it into tomorrow's plan.

TRY IT FREE
Choose monthly or annual support with the exact Google Play price and eligible trial shown before checkout. Renewal terms are stated plainly.

Sugar Light provides general habit support, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it does not guarantee weight loss or other results. Speak with a qualified health professional for personal medical or nutrition advice.
