# Sugar Light Privacy Policy
Effective date: September 17, 2026

Sugar Light helps adults stop added sugar. Your reset date, added sugar pattern, costs, motivations, reasons, streaks, and settings are stored on your device. We do not sell personal data. The app does not require an account and does not send your reset data to our servers in this release.

Google Play processes subscription purchases and provides purchase status to the app. Its handling of payment data is governed by Google's privacy policy. Sugar Light can schedule local notifications on your device; notification text and timing stay on device.

You can delete app data by clearing storage or uninstalling Sugar Light. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play. The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Sugar Light offers behavioral support and general educational milestones; it is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical help or severe symptoms, contact a qualified health professional.
